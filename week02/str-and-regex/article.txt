The sun rose over the quiet town, painting the rooftops in shades of gold and amber. Birds began their morning songs, and the smell of fresh bread drifted from the little bakery on Main Street. It was going to be a good day — maybe even a great one.
Maria checked her phone: 7:45 AM. She had 30 minutes before the train left, just enough time to grab a coffee and finish her well-known notes. She loved mornings like this, when the world felt full of possibility and every street corner seemed to hide a small adventure.
Sometimes the smallest choices lead to the biggest changes. A hello to a stranger, a book picked up by chance, or a detour down an unfamiliar road — who knows where they might take you?
